Official rules
  1. Opening. Each player throws one die; the higher throw moves first using both numbers shown. Ties are re-thrown.
  2. Movement. Ivory runs 24→1, Ebony 1→24. Each die is a separate move; you may move one checker twice or two checkers once.
  3. Points. You may land on an empty point, your own point, or a point holding a single enemy checker (a blot) — which is hit and sent to the bar. Two or more enemy checkers block the point.
  4. Doubles are played four times.
  5. Obligation. Both numbers must be played if legally possible (as many as possible on doubles). If only one can be played, you must play it; if either but not both, you must play the higher.
  6. The bar. Checkers on the bar re-enter in the opponent's home board before any other move. Blocked on both numbers = turn forfeited.
  7. Bearing off begins only when all fifteen checkers are home. An exact number bears off from that point; a number larger than your highest occupied point bears off from that highest point; otherwise it must be played as an ordinary move. Being hit stops bear-off until the checker comes all the way home.
  8. Scoring. 1 point, doubled for a gammon (loser bore off none) and tripled for a backgammon (none off and a checker still on the bar or in the winner's home), multiplied by the cube.
  9. Doubling cube. Before rolling, the cube owner (or either player when centred) may double the stake. The opponent takes — gaining ownership — or drops and concedes the current value.
